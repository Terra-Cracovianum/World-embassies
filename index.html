    About this map

    A Terra Cracovianum instrument. Missions are drawn from OpenStreetMap and refreshed every week, so a move or a new consulate can take a few days to appear.

    This is not an official register. Before you travel, check opening hours and appointments with the mission itself or with the Polish Ministry of Foreign Affairs.
